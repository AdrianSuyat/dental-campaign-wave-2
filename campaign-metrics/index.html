Three things for the tracker

Click rate is 0.00% everywhere because these emails contain no linksThat is how they were written, not a tracking fault and not a performance result. Click rate is simply not a usable metric for this phase, and it should be recorded as not applicable rather than as a zero. Wave 2 changes this: the heatmap image and a link to the competitor listing both go in the body, so clicks become measurable from that send onward.
The cold sequence is bouncing at 12.1%, and that is the one number to act onDeserve Better is at 2.49%, which is healthy. 12.1% is not. Above roughly 2% you start paying for it in sending reputation, and because these campaigns share a sending domain the damage is not contained to the one sequence. Worth a list-hygiene pass before that sequence sends again.
Reply rate and conversions are not tracked, and they are the two that matterDeserve Better opened at 48.98%% and produced no recorded replies. That is the whole finding: people are reading and not acting. Opens tell us the subject line and the sending reputation work. Nothing currently tells us whether the offer does. Reply counts can be pulled manually if a number is needed this week, but it needs setting up properly in GHL.

One pattern worth noting

Tiny is falling across attempts, 20.49%% then 14.75%%, which reads as fatigue. Invisible went the other way, 26.20%% then 33.51%%. Same cadence, opposite direction, so it is worth understanding before either gets another send.

Reintroducing SGEN to people who never paid

Two sequences and 18 reply handlers in one document. Founder access and Sandbox get their own three emails and their own two handlers. The remaining 14 handlers are shared and appear once.

Read this before the copy
  • The job is reintroduction. These people opted in through an ad or early access and never paid. Email 1 reintroduces what they already hold, email 2 teaches one thing they could not have guessed, email 3 leaves the door open. The ask is a person every time, with the product link underneath as the alternative.
  • No unsubscribe or postal block in the body. GoHighLevel appends its own footer to bulk sends, so a second one here would duplicate it. If GHL is not appending one on this workflow, say so and it goes back in.
  • Founder access is closed, per James on 14 August. The founder sequence says so plainly and pivots to the price drop instead: a fully loaded site was $180 a month, it is $39 now, which is a bigger cut than founder access was going to give them. $39 is verified on /pricing, re-read today. $180 is James’s figure from the call and is verified nowhere, so confirm it before this sends.
  • Confirm the two lists are disjoint before either sends. If one contact can hold founder access and a Sandbox, they get two near-identical sequences from the same sender in the same week.

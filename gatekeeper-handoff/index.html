Reissuing wave 1 to the person who decides

Three emails and 8 reply handlers. Coty rings a practice, the front desk gives him the right person, and this goes to that person opening on the call rather than out of nowhere. James’s second priority, ahead of any further waves.

What James asked for, in his words
  • “Coty now talked to somebody, he talked to a gatekeeper, and he got a better contact for who the real decision maker is. So we have, in MediMarketing, fields for gatekeeper and decision maker, DM1, GK1. And based on those fields being entered, the emails would reach out and say, hey, I just talked to GK1, and they told me that this was the best email to reach out, and what I was reaching out about was this concerning XYZ.”
  • “Reissue wave one in a new way. I want that as the priority, as number two priority here.” Ahead of wave 2 and wave 3, which he deprioritised in the same breath.
  • The field names match MediMarketing on purpose, so Lem can lift the automation rather than rebuild it.
The name problem Coty raised, measured

Coty: the name in the emails “looks weird, it’ll be the first name and then some piece of information about the office”. He is right, and it is worse than it sounds.

On the dental book, first_name is literally “Dr” on 344 rows and “Doctor” on 200 more. That is 544 of 1,219, 45%, opening “Hi Dr,”. The greeting column is properly formed: 421 bare first names and 576 in “Dr. Surname” form, blank on 222. So this sequence uses the name Coty writes down on the call, falls back to greeting, and never touches first_name. The build fails if it appears. Every other campaign should be checked for the same thing.

Before it can fire
  • Two fields Coty fills on the call: gatekeeper_name and decision_maker. Neither exists in the data because neither exists until the call happens. Both must be required on the outcome form, because an empty one puts a hole in the first line.
  • It signs Coty, with the footer he supplied. He made the call, so it cannot sign anyone else.
  • The booking link still does not exist as a custom value, and every message here ends on it.
  • Sending is still halted. GoHighLevel caps out at five sending domains and needs a manual switch every 30 emails, so the team is testing Moan and SalesForge instead. Nothing here sends until one of those is working.

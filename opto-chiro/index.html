Before it sends

BLOCKERS
The sending setup, which stops everything
  • Outbound is halted until the 40 warmed addresses are wired up. James, 17 August: cold outreach has been going from reply@sgen.com, a root domain, on one account. Nothing here sends until Lem has multi-sender working.
  • Ramp: 5 to 10 a day per address for the first week or two, 10 to 15 after four or five weeks, never more than 45. At 456 across 40 addresses this sits comfortably inside a first-week ramp.
  • 218 of 456 addresses are verified. The rest are published, catch-all or unverified. On freshly warmed senders, start with the verified ones only.
Smaller, and none of them copy
  • The signature is a merge field on purpose. Each of the 40 sending addresses has its own persona, so a baked-in name would need rewriting 40 times.
  • The heatmap is a link, not an embedded image. Local Falcon serves SVG and mail clients will not render it inline. Rasterising to PNG is what is currently blocking dental wave 2 on hosting, and linking sidesteps it while making click rate measurable for the first time.
  • Handlers marked auto-fire send without a human. Everything else is routed for a person to send, because it either promises a map somebody has to produce or answers a question that deserves a real reply.
  • Wave 2 is not written. Decide it after this has run.

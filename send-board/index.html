What the numbers are telling us

  • Two of the three books have never been touched. Every email so far went to dental. All 818 optometry and 639 chiropractic practices are still cold.
  • 490 practices cannot be emailed by anyone. A third of the optometry and chiropractic books have no address on file. No copy decision fixes that, only enrichment, and it is the largest single block of unreachable prospects we have.
  • Attempt 1 bounced at 12.1%, and that is the one figure to act on. The later attempts run under 5%. Above roughly 2% you pay for it in sending reputation, and the cause is now known: cold sending from a root domain on one account.
  • Click rate is 0.00% everywhere, and that is not a result. None of the sent emails contained a link, so there was nothing to click. Record it as not applicable. Wave 2 puts a competitor link and an image in the body, so clicks become measurable from that send onward.
  • No replies are being tracked. The follow-up opened at 49% and produced no recorded replies. Opens tell us the subject line and the sending reputation work. Nothing currently tells us whether the offer does.
  • One send needs its attempt number confirming. GoHighLevel labels it “Day 8 follow-up” rather than an attempt, so it is shown separately rather than folded into a guess. Lem can settle which attempt it was.

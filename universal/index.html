How one draft covers three verticals. All three master sheets share an identical 130-column schema, and {{contact.search_phrase}} already resolves per vertical: “dentist near me”, “chiropractor near me”, “optometrist near me”. The draft adapts itself.
  • CSV column name collides with the built-in GHL contact name. Import it to a custom field business_name.
  • {{custom_values.booking_link}} is account-level, set once under Settings → Custom Values. It must exist before sending or every handler ships a blank link.
  • Nothing says “practice”. The copy works for a business that is not one.
  • heatmap_svg_url is live for all 1201, so every lead gets their own map. That is the first vertical-wide campaign where that is true.
What one draft is allowed to claim. Tested against every single lead before any copy was written.
nemesis_beats_at_pins ≥ 1TRUE for all . A named rival is ahead of every one of them

So the copy states their own numbers and never sorts them into a segment. The numbers do the work: a business at position 15 reads it as bad news, one at position 5 reads it as close but not there.
